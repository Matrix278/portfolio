About me

Engineering the
things you don’t see.

I’m a software engineer with a focus on backend development: microservices architecture, Go, RPC communication, and cloud services. I develop and maintain scalable applications, working with AWS to connect services and keep data moving.

My frontend experience comes from both professional work and personal projects. With HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Elm, I’ve built websites and interfaces that complement the systems behind them.

I enjoy taking a problem from investigation through implementation, testing, and code review. Collaborating with QA and other developers, writing maintainable code, and learning along the way are central to how I work.
